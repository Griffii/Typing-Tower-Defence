# Leximancer story

## Premise

Saborus steals the Gem of Creation to uncover the true nature of reality. He uses it to push beyond the limits of traditional Spelling magic. His experiments create life, alter nature, manipulate time, and break the laws of physics.

The Player and Jisho pursue him across the kingdom to recover the Gem. They encounter increasingly bizarre creatures and distorted environments. At first, they do not know what Saborus is trying to discover.

Saborus eventually learns that their entire world is an artificial simulation. The discovery disillusions him. He concludes that life in an artificial world has no meaning. He decides to erase reality, believing that he is freeing everyone within it.

The Player and Jisho must stop him. The final confrontation centres on this theme: **even if their world is artificial, the lives, relationships, and experiences within it still have meaning.**

## Saborus's motivation

- **Initial goal:** Discover what reality is and how it works. The theft serves his research.
- **Method:** Use the Gem to test increasingly fundamental rules. Each answer leads to a more dangerous experiment.
- **Harm:** He treats living beings and communities as experimental material. His curiosity does not excuse the damage he causes.
- **Revelation:** The world is a simulation. This is a discovery he makes during the campaign, not knowledge he holds at the start.
- **Fatal conclusion:** He mistakes an artificial origin for a lack of meaning. He decides that erasure is liberation and imposes that decision on everyone.
- **Final conflict:** The Player and Jisho defend the value of their shared lives and everyone's chance to keep living them.

## Campaign arc

| Levels | Story movement | What the Player and Jisho understand |
|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Theft, pursuit, and experiments with new life | Saborus is using the Gem to create creatures. His purpose remains unclear. |
| 4–6 | Experiments spread into living organisms, physical laws, and causality | The damage forms a pattern. Saborus is investigating the rules of the world. |
| 7 | The investigation reaches the boundaries of reality | Proposed reveal point: evidence at the observatory confirms the world is artificial. |
| 8 | Saborus reaches the underlying system | His disillusionment becomes a plan to erase the world. The pursuit becomes a rescue of reality itself. |
| 9 | The final rewrite begins | The Player and Jisho confront Saborus and defend the meaning of life within their world. |

The exact reveal scene and dialogue remain to be written. Keep the simulation revelation out of early dialogue. Early anomalies should first read as strange consequences of Spelling magic.

## Character and tone

Keep Jisho exuberant and the Player restrained. Let humour arise from their reactions and the kingdom's inhabitants. As the threat grows, give the consequences room to land.

Thistle joins after Seaside Farm in the existing dialogue. Keep him present in later story planning. His arrival and the goblins' gratitude give the party a concrete relationship to carry into the finale.

Build the final argument through encounters across the campaign. Creations can make choices. Altered people still have lives worth protecting. Repeated days still contain experiences that matter. These moments should prepare the ending before anyone knows the truth about the world.

The finale should show the party protecting people and places they have come to care about. Do not resolve the conflict by revealing that the simulation was a lie. Who created it, what exists outside it, and Saborus's fate remain open decisions.

## Spelling and discovery

Proposed lore: Spelling disrupts the magical patterns that hold a creature's form together. An enemy's words reflect its nature, training, or altered state. Learning those words makes the pursuit a growing understanding of the world and Saborus's experiments.

Early enemies use familiar vocabulary. Later creatures reveal words about matter, time, boundaries, and eventually the underlying system. Keep that vocabulary consistent with the timing of the simulation reveal.

Encountered pools become part of the player's grimoire and unlock for Endless. See the [progression and economy plan](progression-and-economy.md) for pool examples and discovery rules.
